05Treatment Guide · Actions · Mission Routine

Mission Routine · Reminders · REM-01

MEASUREMENTS + ACTIONS · BUILT INTO THE DAY
  • 06:30
    Morning measurements — blood pressure + glucoseBP cuff seated, arm at heart level, after 5 quiet minutes. Glucose finger-stick before breakfast. Fasting, same-time readings are the ones that compare day to day.
  • 07:00
    Daily check-in — 30 secondsRate mood, energy, stress and sleep quality 1–5 in CHK-01 below. This is where the mood indicator comes from; without it, the console only sees half of you.
  • 12:30
    Midday re-check — only if a morning reading was amber or redRepeat the flagged measurement once, same conditions. One odd reading is noise; two in a row are a signal the rules can act on.
  • 16:00
    Exercise countermeasure — 30 minutesResistive device + cycle ergometer. One session treats four systems at once: heart, bone, muscle and mood. Skipping it is a health decision, not a rest day.
  • 21:30
    Wind-down — protect tonight's sleepLights low, displays off, no caffeine after 14:00. Tonight's sleep sets tomorrow's heart-rate baseline, mood and immune strength.

      Station Schematic · Where the Loop Lives On Board

      SELECT MODULE

      Destiny — U.S. LaboratoryTHE ONBOARD BRAIN

      The station's primary research lab. AstroPulse tie-in: the onboard edge service — ingest, signal processing, rule engine, local store — runs on a crew tablet here, so scoring never waits for a ground round-trip.

      STAGE 01

      INGEST

      BLE 5.0 · CHEST STRAP / PATCH

      ECG at 250 Hz + PPG stream in; packets timestamped to mission clock, sequence-checked.

      IN ecg_raw, ppg_raw
      OUT 1 Hz telemetry frame
      >
      STAGE 02

      SIGNAL PROCESSING

      ON-DEVICE DSP · PAN-TOMPKINS

      R-peak detection yields HR + RR intervals; RMSSD yields HRV. Artifacts rejected before any metric is trusted.

      IN telemetry frame
      OUT hr, hrv, spo2, temp
      >
      STAGE 03

      RISK ENGINE

      DETERMINISTIC RULES · HRP RANGES

      Physiology fused with the daily check-in, scored vs personal baseline. Output: 0–100 risk index + the rule that fired.

      IN metrics + check-in
      OUT risk index, actions
      >
      STAGE 04

      LOCAL STORE

      SQLITE · ENCRYPTED AT REST

      Full-resolution data stays on the vehicle. 90 days of trends onboard; record belongs to the crew member.

      RETENTION 90 days onboard
      ACCESS subject + CMO
      >
      STAGE 05

      DOWNLINK SYNC

      STORE-AND-FORWARD · TDRS / DSN

      Per comm window: compressed trend package — indices, flags, check-ins, no raw ECG. Built for 22-min Mars light delay.

      PKG ~40 KB / sol
      CADENCE per comm window
